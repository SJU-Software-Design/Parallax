# Tutorial → Museum elevator E01

Implemented 2026-11-14. The ordinary tutorial starts at lesson one. `-tutorial-elevator-review` starts at the final sheltered approach for inspection.

The actual E01 glTF source supplies the cabin, four independent door leaves, frames, handrails and buttons. The source mirror plane uses translucent glass and the wall behind it has a real mesh aperture. Added shaft guides and passing lights move upward with a smooth start/stop envelope to convey descent. The physical cabin remains a stable space for the player; a persistent cabin camera hides the asynchronous stage replacement without a black transition screen. Camera position and orientation are transferred relative to the cabin, with one active listener.

Sequence: approach opens the doors; the entire CharacterController must be inside for 0.75 seconds; the doors close over 2.1 seconds; a player or rigidbody in the doorway reopens them; descent lasts 5.2 seconds; the floor changes from 1 to B1 near arrival; a 0.65-second stop precedes the destination opening. Loading must also finish before opening. The exterior and interior displays use depth-tested text. Pause freezes the sequence and restarting the sunlight lesson restores the initial closed elevator. Start/stop shake changes rendering only, not the transferred player pose.

Gallery creates its own controller. During the handoff its camera, menu and gameplay are held until the saved pose is restored. The entrance end wall and its separately generated mouldings are split around a real opening with physical surfaces. The existing first gallery is the arrival destination, and the Dike/bottle balance exhibition remains enabled on this route. The persistent cabin is removed when leaving Gallery for another scene. The selected elevator sound bank uses eight PCM clips and seven cabin-owned voices, including one shared door voice. The voices persist with the cabin across scene loading.
